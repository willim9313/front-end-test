Specimen notes:

Thirteen figures, filed from tallest to smallest and back again. Each wears a dark shell — scales, plates, folds or a long drape — and each has one window cut into it, where something soft is kept: a coil, a heart, a row of ribs. The shells are what you see first; the windows are why they were collected.

十三具標本，從最高排到最矮再排回來。每一具都穿著深色的外殼——鱗片、甲片、褶層或長斗篷——外殼上都開了一扇窗，裡面收著柔軟的東西：一團腸、一顆心、一排肋骨。先看到的是外殼，但收藏它們的理由是那扇窗。
