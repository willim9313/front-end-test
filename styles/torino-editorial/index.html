La città

Turin, Torino in Italian, is the capital of Piedmont. It began as a Roman colony, Julia Augusta Taurinorum, became the seat of the House of Savoy, and served as the first capital of the Kingdom of Italy from 1861 to 1865.[1]

The city gave Italy vermouth, gianduja chocolate and the long afternoons of its historic cafés. The Shroud is kept here too, in the cathedral a few steps from Piazza Castello, under the spire of the Mole Antonelliana.

Turin is also famous for magic and the occult. Legend places it on two magic triangles: a white one with Lyon and Prague, a black one with London and San Francisco. Nostradamus is said to have stayed here in 1556.[2]

Guides will tell you the white heart of the city lies in Piazza Castello, and the black one in Piazza Statuto.
